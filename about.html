About AquaSense

Our Mission

AquaSense uses AI and real-time data to empower individuals and communities to conserve water efficiently. We visualize personal and regional water impact, provide live drought updates, and connect users with sustainable solutions.

Our Vision

A world where every drop counts and every individual has the tools and knowledge to make a meaningful impact on water conservation. We believe technology can bridge the gap between awareness and action.

What We Offer

  • AI-powered water usage tracking and insights
  • Real-time drought and reservoir monitoring
  • Personalized water-saving recommendations
  • Community engagement and conservation projects
  • Interactive maps of water bodies and conservation efforts
